## Bugfixes
- Fixed a bug where _lightning arrows_ recipe gave _lightning essence_ instead of the arrows.

---

## Items
- Added rotten flesh variants for almost all zombie types. These variants are:
  - **_Burnt flesh_**: This flesh is dropped by the Scorched, ignites the player with regular fire for 5 seconds when consumed.
  - **_Pneumatic flesh_**: This flesh is dropped by the Soul Scorched, ignites the player with soul fire for 5 seconds when consumed.
  - **_Frozen flesh_**: This flesh is dropped by the Lost, and applies _slowness_ for 30 seconds when consumed.
  - **_Poisonous flesh_**: This flesh is dropped by the Rotten, and applies _poison_ for 15 seconds when consumed.
- Added _Soul fragment_, a new special item dropped for Soul Reapers.

---

## Recipes
- _Soul arrows_ can now be crafted with 1 arrow and 8 soul fragments.
- _Soul sand_ can now be crafted with 2 sand and 2 soul fragments.
- _Soul soil_ can now be crafted with 2 dirt and 2 soul fragments.

---

## Enchantments
- Added Will o' the Wisp enchantment: Will o' the Wisp is a new enchantment that can be applied to swords (and derivatives added for other mods). Works like vanilla's _Fire Aspect_, but ignites the target on soul fire instead of regular fire.
